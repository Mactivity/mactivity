- **Open to the network**, on the Network tab: the ports other devices on your network can reach, by app, what macOS's own are (AirPlay Receiver, with where to turn it off), and whether the macOS firewall is on.
- **New startup items:** an alert when something adds itself to start with your Mac, naming the app and what it added, even if it put itself back after you turned it off.
- **Who made what's running**, on the CPU tab: each app's developer from its code signature, Apple's and the App Store's, with anything nobody's name is on set apart.
- **Wi-Fi over time:** the signal through the day or the week, and how long it was weak.
- **Network changes:** each switch between Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Personal Hotspot and a VPN, and each drop, with the data used and the apps that used it most. Drops and VPN time show on Your day.
- **A monthly data budget:** set an allowance and reset day to see what's left, the pace you're on, each app's share, and get an alert before you go over.
