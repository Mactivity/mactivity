- **A Security page:** who can do what on your Mac, in one place. Each change is made in System Settings or the browser, and the page takes you there.
- **Protections:** FileVault, Gatekeeper, System Integrity Protection, the firewall and automatic updates, each on or off, with what it protects. Checkup tells you when FileVault, Gatekeeper, System Integrity Protection or automatic security fixes are off.
- **What apps are allowed to do:** which apps have Screen Recording, Full Disk Access, Accessibility and Input Monitoring, with Full Disk Access turned on. On macOS 26 also the camera, the microphone, Automation and your folders. macOS 27 doesn't let other apps read those four, so the page says so and links to each list in System Settings.
- **System extensions:** network filters, VPNs, drivers and security tools that run with the system's own privileges, what each is, and whether it's on.
- **Browser extensions:** Safari's, Chrome's, Edge's, Brave's, Arc's and Firefox's, and what each can reach, in plain words: every website, your tabs, your history, apps on your Mac.
- **Time in calls**, on the Devices page: hours in video and voice calls this week and today, by app and by day.
- **Network interfaces**, on the Network tab: what `ifconfig` shows, in plain words. Every connection on your Mac, what it's for, its addresses, router, DNS, speed and traffic, and a hover on each that says what it means.
- **Safer with Full Disk Access:** Homebrew no longer inherits Mactivity's access when Mactivity runs it. Every release is now checked so no other program can load code into Mactivity.
