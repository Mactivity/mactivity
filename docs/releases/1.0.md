- Mactivity is out of beta: a license is $10, once, for three Macs, at mactivity.app/buy, with a full refund within 14 days if it isn't for you.
- New: **Runaway process alerts.** When a node, python or similar process sits at a full core for 10 minutes, or its memory keeps climbing, Mactivity tells you which one, which project it belongs to and which coding agent started it. Quitting it is one confirmation away; Mactivity never stops anything on its own.
- New: **SSD health.** The Disk tab shows each drive's own report: how much of its rated life is used, how much it has written, and whether it reports any trouble. Checkup speaks up when a drive wears out or reports a problem.
- New: **Reports.** Save the last 7 or 30 days as a two-page PDF from View → Save Report or History's Your week card: the figures, a day-by-day chart, the busiest hour and the apps behind it, and the top apps by every measure.
- New: **Slow drift in Checkup.** Checkup notices an app whose memory has crept up over the past weeks, and a battery that empties faster than it used to.
- Keep Awake is now called **Stay Awake**.
- Ask Mactivity is now off unless you switch it on in Settings → General, and it says plainly that its answers should be checked against the figures.
- A project with many listening ports no longer pushes the menu bar window's Projects page off screen.
- The Battery tab's period switch turns into a menu when the labels don't fit, as in German.
- Agents: Stop now also ends the shells a Claude Code session starts in their own process groups.
- Browser breakdown: Chromium's network service is recognized, and an Electron app's main process is labelled correctly.
- Recordings are saved in full when you quit during one, and a write that fails says so.
- "Unplugged at" finds the right time even after more than a day on battery.
- Clearer wording throughout the app and the website.
