- New: **History for any app.** Right-click an app and choose History… to see its last 7, 30 or 90 days: processor, memory, energy and network over time, how long it was in front, its crashes and alerts, and the hours of the week it's busiest.
- New: **Slowdowns you missed.** Why is my Mac slow? now also lists the times in the last 7 days your Mac struggled, with when, how long, and the apps busiest then. Click an app to see its history.
- New: **What happens after you log in.** Startup shows how long your Mac took to calm down after its last startup and which apps kept it busy, marking the ones that open with your Mac. Checkup mentions it when a login keeps the Mac busy for 5 minutes or more. (Mactivity needs to open at login to see this.)
- About and Settings show just the version, like "1.0.2", without the build number.
- Claude Code shows the Claude icon instead of a blank square, and so does any helper app that ships without an icon of its own.
- Click any app in a history chart's Top apps list to open its history.
