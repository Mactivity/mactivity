- **Devices:** a new page with every display, its resolution and size, what it's set to look like and whether that's scaled, its refresh rate and how it's connected; each port and what's on it; the USB and Thunderbolt devices plugged in; and whether the lid is closed. A button opens Displays settings.
- **Your next Mac knows your desk:** how many external displays you use, how sharp, and how often the lid stays closed. A Mac that lives closed on the desk and rarely runs on battery gets a desktop suggestion, and the advice keeps to chips that drive all your displays: two on a MacBook Air needs M4 or later, three or more a Max chip.
- **Alerts opens on what went off,** by day, with the rules a click away in the toolbar.
