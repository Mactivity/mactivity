- New: **Flagged by macOS.** History shows what macOS itself caught: an app writing tens of gigabytes to disk (which wears the SSD), going past macOS's processor limit again and again, apps macOS closed because memory ran out, and restarts your Mac didn't choose, with what was running just before. Checkup tells you when one happened this week.
- New: **App launch times.** History shows how long your apps take to open, each app's history page says "Opens in 1.4s", and Checkup tells you when one gets noticeably slower.
- Beachballs, counted: hangs macOS recorded for the whole Mac now count too, and Checkup tells you when an app hangs much more than it used to.
- History → Sleep lists your recent sleeps: when, how long, what woke your Mac, and how much battery each used. Checkup tells you when your Mac drained its battery while asleep.
- Charger check: plugged in, the Battery tab says whether your charger covers what your Mac is drawing, or that macOS is holding the battery short of full.
- macOS's own processes say what they're doing, in plain words: Spotlight indexing, Photos analyzing your library, Time Machine backing up.
- Time Machine: Storage shows when your Mac last backed up, and Checkup warns you when automatic backups stop.
- Fixed: plugged in but not charging, the battery read "Full" at any level. It now says "Not charging" until it's nearly full.
