- Temperatures in Celsius or Fahrenheit: Settings → General → Temperature. Automatic follows your Mac's region.
- Energy Impact no longer spikes when short-lived processes start (Xcode builds read as tens of thousands).
- The Battery tab's history says it's showing Energy Impact, with whole numbers on the axis.
- Week and month charts no longer show the same date twice.
- Battery time remaining uses the battery's own estimate, which isn't capped at 20 hours.
- Percentages follow your region's style, like "27 %" in Germany and France.
- Choose what each metric shows in Settings → General → Figures: memory as used, percent or pressure; network as download, upload or both; disk as free space, percent used or activity; battery as percent, time left, both or watts; and which temperature sensor. Automatic keeps today's figures.
- Hide rows you don't need in the menu bar window: Settings → Menu Bar → Menu bar window.
- A Process menu with keyboard shortcuts: Get Info ⌘I, Show in Finder ⇧⌘R, Quit ⌥⌘Q, Force Quit ⌥⌘⌫, plus Quit and Get Info buttons in the toolbar. Mactivity still asks before quitting anything.
- Mactivity tells you when notifications are off, with a button to turn them on.
- A Storage scan now survives switching tabs.
- Desktop Macs get an Energy tab instead of an empty Battery tab.
- "Volume" in the sidebar is now "Sound". Clicking the Dock icon opens the window.
- Mactivity speaks your language: Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Dutch, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil) and Spanish. It picks up the language your Mac is set to. These translations are new; tell us about anything that reads oddly.
- Lighter: about 12% less CPU while idle in the menu bar, faster history charts, and the menu bar window and Settings let go of their views when closed.
