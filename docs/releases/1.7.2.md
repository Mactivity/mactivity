- **Watts over any range** on the Battery tab: what your whole Mac drew, display included, on average and at its peak, over the range you pick in the toolbar, from 12 hours to a year. Mactivity starts recording it with this update, so longer ranges fill in over the coming weeks and say since when until then.
- **Energy use by app follows the same range**, instead of its own 24 hours / 7 days, and shows each app's average watts beside its share.
- **Power reads in watts everywhere**: 0.85 W rather than 850 mW, the way chargers and Apple's specs put it.
- **Figures explain themselves** when you hover: power draw and watts, the charger's rating, cycle count, max capacity, disk speeds, today's totals, and why internet speeds in bits (100 Mbps) are about an eighth of what Mactivity shows in bytes (12.5 MB/s).
