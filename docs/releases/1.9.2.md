- **Much less memory in the background:** closing the main window now really frees what drawing it took. In our measurements Mactivity holds about 42 MB after you've closed the window, down from about 70 MB, at no measurable cost in CPU. If the window hasn't been opened since launch, it holds about 20 MB, down from about 28 MB.
- **The main window stays where you put it**, after you close it and now after you quit too. If the display it was on is gone, it opens centered on one you have.
