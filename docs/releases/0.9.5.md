- Mactivity now needs a license: $10 once, for up to three of your Macs. Until you paste your key, the window and the menu bar show only the license screen, and nothing is sampled or recorded. Licenses are by invitation during the private beta.
- A year of history. Mactivity keeps 15-minute detail for 30 days and hourly detail for a year, and History adds 90-day and 1-year ranges. A year takes about 40 to 60 MB; change it in Settings → History.
- At 30 days, the busiest apps show how they moved against the 30 days before.
- Search finds processes and PIDs too, not just apps.
- Hover a figure like Wired, Compressed or Load average to see what it means.
- Mute an app's alerts from its row, or from the notification itself.
- Right-click a dev server to open it in your browser, in Terminal or in Finder, or copy its URL.
- Storage: press Space for Quick Look, ⌘⌫ to move the selected item to the Trash, ⌘R to rescan, or drag an item into Finder.
- VoiceOver reads chart summaries. With Differentiate Without Color on, statuses are shown as words.
