- No more keychain password prompts: Mactivity keeps your license in a part of the keychain that belongs to the app, so it never has to ask. The first launch moves it over, asking at most once if macOS insists.
- Share makes an image of Overview's cards with their live graphs. Send it from the Share menu, copy it, or save it to Downloads.
- The year at a glance: History opens with a calendar of the last year, one square per day, for CPU, GPU, memory, disk or network. Click a day to see its hours and its busiest apps.
- Export History… in Settings → History saves every hour kept, for every app, as a CSV file for a spreadsheet.
- Accessories: History shows the battery of your AirPods, Magic Mouse, Keyboard and Trackpad over time, when each was last charged, and about how long it has left.
- History keeps your Mac's battery level (with how many times it charged), memory pressure, swap, and free space, with a forecast of the day the disk fills up at the current pace. Data used shows what each app sent and received.
- Sleep: see what's keeping your Mac awake right now (also on the Battery tab), a week of when it slept, and what woke it.
- The Disk tab shows data read and written since startup, today, this week, this month and this year, the read and write counts, and which apps wrote the most today.
- Crashes and hangs per app, from macOS's own reports on your Mac, and time in front: how long you used each app.
- New alerts: an accessory battery low, the disk filling up, an app that keeps crashing, and a Mac that stayed awake all night. Each can be turned off in Settings → Alerts.
- Checkup: a short list of what's worth doing on your Mac, from a filling disk to an app that keeps crashing, each with a button to fix it.
- Battery use by app, like the iPhone's: switch the Battery tab's list to Today or 7 days to see what used your battery, plus battery health and when it'll reach 80%.
- The Battery tab's chart shows your battery level, and says how much you used and charged ("Used 90%, charged 60%"). Top apps show how energy divided up between them.
- Your week: time in use, sleep, data and crashes for the last seven days, as a card on History, a shareable image, and a Monday notification.
- Uninstall App… in Storage moves an app and the files it left in your Library to the Trash, after showing you everything first.
- Fixed: top apps ranked apps that ran for a few seconds, like Phone, above apps used all day.
