- New: **After updates.** History compares each macOS and app update of the last month with the week before it: "Google Chrome 155: Memory 1.2 GB → 1.6 GB (+33%)", or about the same as before. Checkup tells you when an update made something heavier.
- New: **Compare.** Put any two days, or two weeks, side by side: time in use, slowdowns, alerts, processor, memory, power, data and disk writes, and the apps that changed most. From Your day's Compare… button, or type "compare" in Quick Search.
- New: **What grew.** After a storage scan, Mactivity names the folders that grew most since the last scan of the same place, like "DerivedData +9.2 GB", and opens each on the map.
- New: **Build times.** Mactivity times every build by project, from the compilers it sees (Xcode and Swift, Rust, Go, C and C++), and tells you when a project's builds got slower: History → Build times, Checkup, and `mactivity builds` for your coding agent.
- New: **Control Center controls.** Add Clean Keyboard and Stay Awake to Control Center or the menu bar.
- Battery health month by month: History → Battery health → By Month shows each month's capacity and the cycles it used, and the card shows cycles a month. The forecast to 80% now works from wear per cycle too, so it no longer waits for capacity to move.
