- Checkup is where you already look: its summary sits on Overview and in the menu bar window, which also says when an app is keeping your Mac awake and shows your accessories' battery levels.
- Checkup tells you when Mactivity's notifications are turned off, since every alert and the weekly summary depend on them.
- Power by app now measures each app's own energy. It read close to zero before.
- On the power adapter, Power draw shows what your Mac is drawing instead of 0, and Source names the adapter's wattage.
- Energy Impact lists show each app's share as a percentage, and the Now / Today / 7 days switch stays on the right.
- Disk totals count everything since your Mac started up, even from before Mactivity began recording, and say "at least" where history doesn't reach back far enough. Read and write counts are short and readable ("35.3M reads").
- Totals over a month or a year (data written, data used, time on battery) no longer overcount hours your Mac slept through part of.
- History's charts always span their whole range, and no longer flatten your data under a far-off alert line.
- Overview's cards fill every row on wide windows.
- Apps are grouped better: the iOS Simulator's helpers count as Simulator, Xcode's device services as Xcode, and app extensions like Excel's widget as their app.
- Fewer false alarms: crash alerts and Checkup count crashes of apps, not of command-line tools and test helpers; Checkup no longer suggests quitting parts of macOS; and a growing-memory alert repeats only once the app has grown again.
- Your week says when time in use started recording, instead of calling a few hours a whole week.
