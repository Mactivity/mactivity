- **Your month and Your year**, in History → Looking back: time in use against the month or year before, the busiest day (and month), the apps you used most, the ones new this time and the ones you stopped using, your agents' hours and the project they worked in most, slowdowns, data used and written to disk. Go back month by month or year by year, and share either as an image. Through December it opens on Your year.
- **Notes on your history:** add a note to a moment, like "Installed the new driver", from Your day. Notes show on Your day, the Today so far ribbon and widget, and every History chart. Click one to change or delete it. They stay on your Mac until you delete them.
- **Heat by app**, in History: the apps that take more of the processor when your Mac is warm than they usually do, from the last four weeks. A Mac that stays cool is told so.
- **Charging habits**, in History on a Mac with a battery: how long it sits plugged in at full, how much of its charging happens while it's warm, and how often it runs down under 10%, with what would be easier on the battery. Advice only: Mactivity never changes how your Mac charges.
