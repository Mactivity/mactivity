- New: **Out of the ordinary.** Mactivity learns each app's usual day on your Mac and tells you when today isn't: "Safari: 5.5 GB of memory today, 4× its usual 1.3 GB." On Overview, in Checkup, and as a notification when an app reaches 4× its usual day (once a day per app; change it or turn it off in Settings → Alerts).
- New: **Your day.** Pick any day on History's calendar and Mactivity tells it as a timeline: when your Mac woke and slept, busy stretches and slowdowns with the apps behind them, unplugging and charging, big downloads, alerts and crashes.
- New: **Agent history.** Mactivity now remembers your coding agents' sessions. Agents → This week shows how long Claude Code, Codex and the rest worked in each project, day by day, and what they cost in processor and memory; Your day shows each session on the timeline.
- New: **Today so far** on Overview: your day on one line, from midnight to now, with a button into Your day.
- New: **Rewind.** Click any bar in a History chart to see the apps behind that moment: what was using the processor at 3 AM, or the memory during last Tuesday's slowdown. Hover to read any bar's figures.
- Network and Disk show today's totals: received and sent, read and written since midnight.
- Storage's category legend lists every category, wrapping onto a second line when needed, and Your week's top apps wrap in narrow windows instead of being cut off.
- In Get Info, the CPU and Memory labels sit centered on their graphs.
- Each app's history page shows today beside its usual day on your Mac.
- New in the `mactivity` command and its MCP server: `mactivity agents` (agent history by project), `mactivity day` (any day's timeline) and `mactivity unusual` (what's out of the ordinary today), so your coding agent can ask what happened.
- New: **Clean Keyboard.** Press ⌃⌥K (or View → Clean Keyboard) and every key does nothing while you wipe the keyboard, until ⌃⌥K again, Unlock, or five minutes. The first time, macOS asks to allow Mactivity in Accessibility.
- Back and Forward between pages: ⌘[ and ⌘] in the View menu.
- Many small fixes so links land where they point: alerts and Checkup open the right card or cleanup, app names everywhere open their history, Esc closes information sheets, a recording shows in the menu bar window, and the View menu's Stay Awake matches the menu bar's.
- Settings and the menu bar window, tidied: shortening history asks first, alert rules explain themselves and can be reset, ⌘, and ⌘1–⌘8 work in the menu bar window, and Stay Awake says why it stopped.
- Ask Mactivity (optional, Apple Intelligence on your Mac) can answer about any day this week and about your coding agents: "What happened on my Mac yesterday?", "What did my agents work on?"
- New: a **Today so far** desktop widget, your day on one line, refreshed every 15 minutes.
