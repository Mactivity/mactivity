- **Big files you forgot**, in Storage → Clean Up: files of 100 MB or more you haven't opened, changed or added in a year, anywhere in your home folder, with each one's last use. Library, app bundles, your projects and their build folders are left alone, and nothing moves to the Trash until you confirm.
- **Duplicate files:** copies of the same file across your folders, matched by their contents, not their names. The copy in Downloads is ticked for the Trash first, and copies that share their space on disk with another aren't offered, since trashing them frees nothing.
- **Cloud folders, sized:** how much iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive and Box keep downloaded on this Mac, by folder. Remove Download keeps the files in the cloud and frees the space here.
- **More in Shortcuts and Siri:** ask how your day went, what's out of the ordinary, how your builds are doing this week, and how long your agents worked. Each comes back as a spoken summary.
- **Interactive widgets:** a Stay Awake switch for the desktop, and Today so far opens Your day.
- **Your own shortcuts**, in Settings → General: choose the keys for Quick Search, Clean Keyboard, Stay Awake and any page of the app. ⌃⌥K always unlocks Clean Keyboard, whatever you choose.
- **Quiet while you present:** alerts wait while you're on a call, presenting in Keynote or PowerPoint, or mirroring your display, then arrive as one notification that opens Alerts. A new startup item always comes through, and nothing waits more than two hours. Turn it off in Settings → Alerts.
- **Compare two Macs**, on History: save this Mac's report as a file, open it in Mactivity on another Mac, and see the two side by side: processor, memory, swap, heat, disk writes, downloads and the busiest apps. It's a file you move yourself; nothing is uploaded.
- The Disk tab's totals add up: Today and 7 days showed 0 B on a Mac that had been running since before the span began, and 30 days and the year left out everything read and written since startup.
