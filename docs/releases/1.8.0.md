- **Camera and microphone history**, on the Devices page: which apps used the camera, a microphone, recorded the screen or read your location in the last week, how long, and the latest times, with a video call's camera and microphone as one. It comes from the dots macOS shows in the menu bar while an app uses one, and stays on your Mac.
- **On Your day too:** "FaceTime used the camera and microphone", with when it started and stopped.
- **Downloads you never opened**, in Storage → Clean Up: files at the top of Downloads that macOS has no record of being opened since they arrived, a week ago or more, with their sizes. Nothing goes to the Trash until you confirm. Installers, archives and apps are left out.
- **Lighter still:** the new camera and microphone history checks every 10 seconds at about a ten-thousandth of a percent of one core, and reads macOS's record only when something changes.
